Case's Physics Breakdown: Driven vs. Parametric Oscillator

STEP 01
🛑

Zero External Force (Rest)

The swing hangs vertically (θ = 0°). Feet dangle in mid-air (no ground contact). Both linear and angular momentum about the pivot are zero.

STEP 02
🔄

Internal Body Rotation (φ)

The rider rotates their torso back and extends legs forward. This changes body angle φ(t). By conservation of angular momentum, the swing chair must rotate in response: τinertial = -Ibody φ¨.

STEP 03
⚖️

Gravitational Tilt Torque

Tilting the body shifts the center of mass away from the suspension line by distance d·sin(φ). Downward gravity immediately exerts a driving torque: τtilt = -m·g·d·sin(θ+φ). Motion starts!

STEP 04
📈

Resonant Energy Transfer

The swinger rocks their body at the natural frequency ω0. Net power P(t) = D(t)·ω > 0 continuously transfers muscular energy into mechanical swing energy, rapidly amplifying the swing arc.

CASE & SWANSON (1990) EQUATION OF MOTION

Why Your Teacher Was Wrong:
At dead stop (θ = 0, θ̇ = 0): as soon as the rider activates core muscles to tilt their body (φ ≠ 0 or φ¨ ≠ 0), the driving term D(0) ≠ 0.
Therefore, θ¨(0) = D(0) / Itotal ≠ 0. The swing must begin moving!
